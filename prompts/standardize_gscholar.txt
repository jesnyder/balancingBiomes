## Add DOI and DOI url

For each artcile in the deduplicated list
add two keys:

initialize as set equal to blank

Search for the doi number in the "pub_url" field using these rules

A DOI (Digital Object Identifier) is a permanent, standardized identifier used to uniquely identify scholarly works such as journal articles, datasets, and reports. It consists of two main parts separated by a slash: a prefix and a suffix. The prefix always begins with 10, which designates the DOI system, followed by a number that identifies the publisher or registrant (for example, 10.1016 for Elsevier or 10.1038 for Nature). The suffix is assigned by the publisher and uniquely identifies the specific item; it often encodes information such as the journal name, year of publication, and article number, but its structure is not standardized. When combined, the prefix and suffix form a globally unique identifier that can be resolved through https://doi.org/, ensuring persistent access to the referenced work even if its web location changes.

Consider these rules:
* DOI designation always begins with 10

* It consists of two main parts separated by a slash: a prefix and a suffix

* Combine prefix and suffix with a slash:

* Prefix format Always starts with 10. (this indicates the DOI system). Followed by a publisher/registrant number:
    Assigned by a DOI registration agency (CrossRef, DataCite, etc.)
    Usually 4–9 digits
    Example prefixes:
    10.1000 → example publisher
    10.1016 → Elsevier
    10.1038 → Nature

* Suffix format is not strictly standardized; can include letters, numbers, periods, dashes, and sometimes slashes Example suffixes:
    9780470015902.a0000394.pub3 → Wiley
    fmicb.2018.00148 → Frontiers in Microbiology
    978-3-319-66480-4 → Springer

* ".pdf" is never part of the doi
* "/full" is never part of the doi

The doi can be at the end of middle of a url.

using doi or title if doi not available
if a result is found, populate the article "abstract" key with the abstract found in OpenAlex.
Pause 2 seconds before making the request to avoid becoming blocked.
